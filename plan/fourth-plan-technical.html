How Clay Bay would be built

The learning engine is the first plan's (the skill map, five steps for every skill, the feedback rules, stars for first tries, reviews, the parents' page; see its technical page). This page covers what is new here: the clay look, the hands-on moves, how answers are checked and what is saved.

1 · The clay look

Three layers of clay

WhatHow it is madeWhy
ScenesPainted pictures (Gemini drafts now; an illustrator or cleaned-up drafts later), one per place, with soft parallax layers where needed.Rich clay texture costs nothing at run time: it is just a picture.
CharactersEach friend and the avatar is drawn as separate lumps (body, eyes, pupils, arms, legs, clothes) and rigged in Rive with bones and soft mesh bending.Clay figures are built from lumps anyway, so they come apart cleanly; squash and stretch suit clay.
The mathsShapes, critters, cakes, weights, number lines and digits are drawn by the app, with one shared "clay" shading (a soft rounded edge, a fingerprint texture, a highlight and a shadow).Counts and sizes must be exact and change every round; drawing them keeps them exact.

The stop-motion feel

  • Characters move on twelve steps a second, like real stop-motion, while the screen itself stays smooth (60 a second) so touches feel instant.
  • A gentle "boil": two or three slightly different fingerprint textures swap now and then, so the clay looks hand-made.
  • Every touch leaves a soft dent that slowly fills back in. It is drawn on a light layer over the scene and costs very little.
  • Reduced motion (a setting): no boil, no camera moves, dents fade at once.
2 · Touch

Eight moves, each with a one-finger way

MoveHow it is recognisedOne-finger way
Press with several fingertipsTouches that land within 0.3 seconds count as one press; touches closer than 8 mm merge; a large contact (a palm) is ignored.tap one at a time
Pull outA drag that starts on the clay's edge and moves outward more than 20 px.the same drag with a mouse
Rub smoothThree or more back-and-forth strokes over a corner.tap the corner
Swipe to cutA quick straight stroke across the whole object; the cut follows the best straight line through it.drag a cut line, or tap two guide dots
Squish / pinch apartDropping one piece onto another joins them; two fingers spreading on a piece splits it.drag one onto the other; tap and choose a split
Stretch a cornerA drag on a block's corner handle snaps to whole rows and columns.+ and − buttons
TwistTwo touches turning around their middle; snaps to quarter turns.the ↻ button
Pinch to zoomTwo touches moving apart or together; marks appear when they are at least 24 px apart.+ and − buttons, arrows
Fingers at once: most tablets read 10 touches at once (iPads 11); some cheap phones only 5; a laptop with a mouse reads one. Poke Pals asks for at most 5 per hand and always accepts one at a time, so it works everywhere. The client's real devices should be tested in the first week.

4 · What is saved

Small recipes, not pictures

A critter

Its body shape, colour and the number of each part, plus a name: a few dozen bytes. The app draws it again from the recipe, so a bay can hold hundreds.

The day

Which stops are done, the egg's warm stones, the Hatch's answers and points, today's hatchling recipe, and any half-done question exactly as it was.

Speed

Every fact's last ten answers and times (for the Scale Coat), and one number per day for Giants' Row.

5 · Test robots

Every activity plays itself

Each activity has a test robot that reads the question (never the answer stored in the code), makes the moves a child would (including several-finger presses, pinches and swipes), checks where everything landed, and makes one planned mistake per session to check the tip. The robots run on every change, at every age.
