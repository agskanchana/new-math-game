Today's challenge: the Dive

The last stop of every day's road: ten questions from what the child has already learned, easier to harder, the later ones worth more, and a card to collect each day. Three looks were drawn. My pick is the Dive: going deeper reads as "harder" at once, it looks nothing like the road on the home screen, and one tall sea picture works for every age.

The rules

How it works

Rule
Where it sitsThe last stop of the day's road, after the three activities. It can also be done later in the day.
Ten stopsEasier to harder. Stops 1–5 are worth 1 point (shells), 6–8 are worth 2 (pearls), 9–10 are worth 3 (gems): 17 at most. Untimed.
Which questionsOnly skills the child has learned, from about three parts of maths (for example numbers, shapes, measures). Harder means less help or two steps, not suddenly bigger numbers.
TriesTwo for every age. Only the first try earns points. After a second miss: the worked steps, then a fresh twin. Every question finished moves the dive on.
Stars10 of 10 first tries = 3 stars; 8–9 = 2; 6–7 = 1; fewer = a tick for finishing.
The cardFinishing the ten earns the day's card for the month's album, plus 20 coins.
Missed daysAn empty slot, nothing lost, no streak message. Older dives can still be done for their cards (no extra coins).
Ages 3–5Two halves of five, at different times of the day, with five dots for each half; both halves earn the card.
Harder levelsTwo days at 14 of 17 or more can suggest a harder level of what is already learned.
