The game

Fizz sits by the bay's sleepy volcano. A fact appears and the child types the answer on the number pad. Every right answer is a fireball Fizz breathes into the crater; a quick answer makes a bigger pink puff, and the rumble meter fills. A miss: the fireball fizzles, the answer shows at once and the fact comes back soon. At the end of the round the volcano POPS: a fountain of popcorn as high as the round went, past the flag of your best.

Hands-on

Tap the number pad, then OK.

The end of a round

POP! A fountain of popcorn rains on the beach, higher than your best, and a crown for a new best.

How it plays

A storyboard of the game in one lesson, drawn from the app's own clay pictures.

1Ready?

The volcano is asleep; the rumble meter is empty; the flag shows your best round.

🔊 “Let's wake up the volcano! Every right answer is a fireball.”

2A fireball

7 × 8 = 56: Fizz breathes a fireball into the crater.

3Quick: a big puff

Right and quick: a big pink puff, and the meter rises.

4A miss

6 × 7 is not 48: the fireball fizzles; the answer shows and the fact comes back soon.

5Rumble!

Eight right: the volcano rumbles, past your best.

6POP!

The popcorn fountain goes higher than your best: a crown.

The quick round (from age 6)

Volcano Pop IS the quick round: ten facts against your best at 6 and 7, sixty seconds from 8. At 5 there is no clock.

The form

The clock

none at 5; ten facts against your best at 6-7; 60 seconds from 8

Help

The keys

0-9 and OK; a decimal point and a fraction bar for older children
