Bridge Builders

The game

The friends want to cross a stream. The child measures the gap (with clay blocks, later a ruler), rolls out a plank of clay that shows its length as it grows, and lays it across. Too short and it plops into the water; the right length and everyone crosses. At the end the day's planks lie on a line plot.

Hands-on

Lay the ruler or blocks across, roll the clay out, drop the plank.

The end of a round

Today's planks on a line plot: most bridges were 14 cm long.

The quick round (from age 6)

The gap or the plank is shown with its ruler: type the length, the difference or the length in other units. Ten questions at 6-7, a 60-second round from 8; your time and your best on screen; only right answers count; a miss shows the answer and comes back later. No clock under 6.

What changes from lesson to lesson

The tool

by eye, clay blocks, a centimetre ruler, inches with halves, a metre stick

The gap

whole units, then halves and quarters, then metres and centimetres

Help

the plank shows its length while rolling; a miss shows by how much

How you answer

rolling and dropping; the number pad
