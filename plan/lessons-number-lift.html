The game

Digsy's burrow is a tall building under the ground. To reach a room the child sets the lift's dials (hundreds, tens, ones; later thousands and tenths) and the lift rides down the shaft; a wrong digit opens a funny wrong room (470 is the sock room, 407 has Fizz's drum). The shaft has a number line beside it, so 10 more, jumps, rounding to the nearest station and decimals between two floors can all be seen. The ride down is a parallax scroll: the shaft's rails rush past, the rooms pass more slowly and the rock far behind, with its gems and fossils, slowest of all, so it feels deep.

Hands-on

Turn the dials, pull the lever; drag the lift along the line.

The end of a round

Every room found today lights up on Digsy's burrow map.

The quick round (from age 6)

The lift is at a floor: type where it ends after a jump (245 + 30), or which station is nearest. Ten questions at 6-7, a 60-second round from 8; your time and your best on screen; only right answers count; a miss shows the answer and comes back later. No clock under 6.

What changes from lesson to lesson

The dials

tens and ones, then hundreds, thousands, a decimal point and tenths

The line

every floor, then every ten, every hundred, or tenths between two floors

Help

the wrong room is funny, not wrong: Digsy points at the digit that sent you there

How you answer

the dials; the number pad for the quick round
