Poke Pals: a new ride instead of the double-decker

The Peeps still need ten seats as two fives (that is what makes "5 and 3 is 8" easy to see), but the seats no longer sit inside a boxy wagon. You chose the roller coaster: when it is full, it rides the loop.

Roller coaster

Its storyboard →

Two cars of five seats: the first car fills, then the second, so 6 is "5 and 1". When all ten are in, it rides the loop!

Big wheel

Ten gondolas, five orange and five blue. When every one has a Peep in it, the wheel turns.

Dune sledge

Two rows of five cushions on a big sledge. When it is full: whoosh, down the dune and into the sea.

Beach bus

An open-top bus with one deck and two rows of five seats. When it is full, it drives off along the promenade.

Races: you against a friend

You drive one kart, a friend drives the other, and the maths drives your kart: a right answer is a boost, the right road, a quicker pit stop or a higher gear.

A close race, every time

The friend drives at the speed of your own best, so races are always close. Beat it, and next time the friend is a little quicker. Really, you are racing yourself.

Kind when you lose

The friend says "So close! Again tomorrow?", never "you lost". A miss shows the answer and the kart keeps going.

By age

At 5: the pit stop, no clock. At 6 and 7: quick races, 10 answers. From Grade 3 the boost race stays as the quick facts warm-up, taking turns with Volcano Pop.

One game, many lessons

Your four races are one game, Kart Cup: the same karts and track, and each lesson picks its race: boosts for facts, pit stops for making 10, the hill for counting in steps, ramps for measuring.
