The game

Friends come to Digsy's cave shop to buy treasures: shells, keys, crystals. The child adds the prices, pays with the right coins (dragging them to the tray), gives change by counting up, and at closing time stacks the day's sales into a bar graph. The coins follow the family's country.

Hands-on

Drag coins to the tray, tap treasures, stack the sales.

The end of a round

Closing time: the day's sales stack up into a bar graph.

How it plays

A storyboard of the game in one lesson, drawn from the app's own clay pictures.

1An order

Pom wants the shell (35¢) and the key (20¢).

🔊 “The shell and the key, please!”

2How much?

The quick round: 35 + 20 = 55. Timed.

3Pay

Drag coins to the tray: 25 + 25 + 5.

4Too much

60 cents is 5 too many: 5 come back.

🔊 “That is 60 cents: 5 too many. Here are 5 back!”

5Change

Mumba pays with a dollar: count up from 55 to 100: 45 cents.

6Closing time

The day's sales as a bar graph.

🔊 “How many more shells than keys did we sell?”

The quick round (from age 6)

Type the total or the change (35 + 20; change from a dollar; 3 × $1.25). Ten questions at 6-7, a 60-second round from 8; your time and your best on screen; only right answers count; a miss shows the answer and comes back later. No clock under 6.
