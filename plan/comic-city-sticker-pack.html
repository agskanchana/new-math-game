Sticker Pack

Muddle jammed the sticker factory, and the stickers came out loose. Ten loose stickers pack into a strip with a CLAP, ten strips into a sheet, ten sheets into a book. The board below counts books, sheets, strips and stickers: thousands, hundreds, tens and ones. Orders must be packed exactly, and when there are not enough strips, a sheet is torn into ten strips (RIIIP): regrouping.

Grades 2–5 · ages 7–10 hero: Mega panel power: loop ten to pack them; tear to unpack

The maths: Place value to 10,000 (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands); reading, writing and comparing numbers; packing and unpacking (regrouping) for adding and taking away. One activity is five rounds, about two minutes. Everything works with one finger or a mouse.

🎉 The fun

Ten loose stickers snap into a strip with a CLAP, and a sheet tears into ten strips with a RIIIP.

🎥 The view

The factory's packing table above; the board of books, sheets, strips and stickers below.

🎆 The finale

Mega grows ten times bigger and carries the delivery over the rooftops (3 seconds).

Storyboard

Six moments

1Stickers everywhere

Muddle jammed the factory: 46 loose stickers on the packing table. The board below shows 0, 0, 0, 0.

🔊 Muddle jammed the machine! Ten loose stickers make a strip. Pack them!

A loop drawn round ten stickers (or ten taps) packs them.

2CLAP!

Four strips and six loose stickers: 46.

🔊 Four strips and six: forty-six!

The board counts strips and stickers as they are packed: tens and ones.

3The order

1 book, 3 sheets, 4 strips and 6 stickers: 1,346.

🔊 One thousand, three hundred and forty-six!

Ten of anything packs into one of the next: the same rule at every place.

4A wrong try

4 sheets and 3 strips were packed (1,436). The delivery check shows both numbers and marks the sheets.

🔊 Look at the sheets: how many hundreds did the school ask for?

Swapped digits are a common slip. The check shows both numbers; the column is marked, never red.

5Unpack!

Deliver 527 when there are 6 sheets and no strips: RIIIP, one sheet becomes ten strips, so 2 strips can go.

🔊 Unpack a sheet: ten strips! Now we can take 2 strips.

Regrouping, the step behind written taking away, as tearing a sheet into strips.

6Delivered

Mega grows ten times bigger and carries the order over the rooftops.

🔊 1,346 delivered!

Mock-ups: Gemini's comic pictures (scenes, heroes, things) with the maths drawn by code in the same inked look, the way the app would draw it, so every count, clock and colour is exact.

The five rounds

What is asked, for one age

  1. Pack 46 loose stickers
  2. Pack the order: 1,346
  3. The delivery check: 1,436 packed for 1,346
  4. Deliver 527 from 6 sheets (unpack)
  5. What is 10 more than 1,346? 100 more?
